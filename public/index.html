Mathematical Model — Dual-Monod Digital Twin

Growth Kinetics
Dual-Monod Growth Rate
$$\mu = \mu_{\max} \cdot \frac{S}{K_S + S} \cdot \frac{C_L}{K_O + C_L}$$
Specific growth rate limited simultaneously by substrate S (g/L) and dissolved oxygen C_L (g/L). Both terms are Michaelis-Menten saturation functions.
Mass Balance
DO Mass Balance (ODE)
$$\frac{dC_L}{dt} = k_La \cdot (C_L^* - C_L) - \text{OUR}$$
k_La = volumetric O₂ mass transfer coefficient (h⁻¹). C_L* = saturation DO. OUR = oxygen uptake rate (g/L/h). Fault regimes mutate k_La or OUR.
Transfer Rates
OUR & CER
$$\text{OUR} = \left(\frac{\mu}{Y_{X/O}} + m_O\right) X$$
$$\text{CER} = \left(\frac{\mu}{Y_{X/C}} + m_C\right) X$$
Y_XO, Y_XC = yield coefficients. m_O, m_C = maintenance coefficients. X = biomass concentration (g/L).
Diagnostics
Respiratory Quotient
$$\text{RQ} = \frac{\text{CER}}{\text{OUR}}$$
Healthy aerobic metabolism: RQ ≈ 1.0. Anaerobic overflow (kLa fault): RQ > 1.0. Contamination: RQ diverges from stoichiometric baseline.
Mass Balance
Biomass & Substrate ODEs
$$\frac{dX}{dt} = (\mu - D)\,X$$
$$\frac{dS}{dt} = -\frac{\mu}{Y_{X/S}}\,X + D\,(S_F - S)$$
D = dilution rate (h⁻¹). S_F = feed substrate concentration. Y_XS = biomass-on-substrate yield. All four ODEs integrated simultaneously via scipy.solve_ivp.
Intervention
kLa–RPM Scaling (Van't Riet)
$$k_La^{\,\prime} = k_La \cdot \left(\frac{\text{RPM}^{\prime}}{\text{RPM}}\right)^{0.6}$$
Applied in the +150 RPM counterfactual rollout. Power-law exponent 0.6 follows the Van't Riet correlation for stirred-tank bioreactors.
Recoverability
Kinetic Stress λ(t)
$$\lambda(t) = w_1\,(C_L - C_L^{\text{crit}})^2 + w_2\,(\text{RQ} - \text{RQ}_0)^2$$
Weighted sum of squared deviations from healthy operating point. w₁, w₂ are tuned weight coefficients. Higher λ = faster recoverability loss.
Recoverability
Recoverability R(t)
$$R(t) = \exp\!\left(-\int_0^t \lambda(\tau)\,d\tau\right)$$
R(t) ∈ (0, 1]. R = 1 → fully recoverable. PNR threshold R_PNR = 0.10. Once R ≤ R_PNR the batch is economically unrecoverable.
Decision
DIW — Decision Intervention Window
$$\text{DIW} = \frac{\ln(R_{\text{now}} / R_{\text{PNR}})}{\lambda_{\text{now}}}$$
Time remaining (hours) until PNR at the current stress rate. Linear extrapolation assumption (hackathon). If λ ≈ 0 the batch is indefinitely stable.
Economics
Cost of Delay
$$\text{CoD}(\Delta t) = V_{\text{batch}}\left(1 - R_{\text{now}}\,e^{-\lambda \Delta t}\right) - C_{\text{interv}}$$
Net economic cost of waiting Δt hours before intervening. Positive CoD → act now is better. V_batch = expected batch revenue. Hackathon assumption on constants.

Fault Regime Reference

✅ Healthy
DO: Stable, 2–8 mg/L
RQ: ≈ 1.0 (aerobic stoichiometry)
Signal: All ODEs within envelope
Action: Continue monitoring
⚠️ kLa Limitation
Onset: t = 8 h (severity 0.7)
DO: Sags post-onset
RQ: Rises (anaerobic shift)
Action: +150 RPM / ↑ airflow
🔥 Substrate Overfeeding
Onset: t = 6 h (severity 3.0)
DO: Crashes (excess OUR demand)
S: Accumulates above normal
Action: -30% feed rate immediately
☣️ Contamination
Onset: t = 5 h (severity 0.45)
RQ: Diverges from stoichiometric
Signal: DO/CER decoupling
Action: Bleed + resterilise
